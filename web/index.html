A duel of memory & sabotage

Pirate Tapper— showdown —

⚓ Two pirates, one screen. Each of ye taps on yer own half — sit facing each other.
⚓ Buttons surface one by one — watch the order they appear. Then pop them in that same order. No numbers, no hints: only the faces, and yer memory.
⚓ Every correct pop cannonballs the enemy's health. Too slow to clear a wave? −30. Wrong button or bare deck? −10.
⚓ Choose yer pirate. Each has a quick trick on a cooldown, and an ultimate charged by clearing waves — the faster and cleaner the clear, the more it charges.
The marks of the deep
Best on a touchscreen laid flat between two players · mouse works for practice · sound on!
